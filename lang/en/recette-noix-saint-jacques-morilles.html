Scallops with morels

35 minServes 4Refined

Sweet, delicate scallops paired with a light morel cream sauce.

Ingredients

  • 16 sea scallops
  • 30 g dried morels
  • 200 ml cream
  • 1 leek, 100 ml white wine and 25 g butter

Method

  1. Soak the morels and soften sliced leek in 10 g butter.
  2. Add morels and wine, reduce for 5 minutes, then pour in the cream.
  3. Sear the scallops in the remaining butter for 2 minutes per side.
  4. Serve the scallops with the sauce for four.
