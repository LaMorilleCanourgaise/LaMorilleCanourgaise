Morel baked eggs

25 minServes 4Delicate

A runny centre and fragrant cream make a warm, refined lunch.

Ingredients

  • 4 fresh eggs
  • 30 g dried morels
  • 150 ml cream, 1 shallot and 20 g butter
  • Salt, pepper and chives

Method

  1. Soak and rinse the morels, then cook with the shallot in butter.
  2. Add cream and reduce for 5 minutes.
  3. Divide between four ramekins and crack in one egg each.
  4. Bake in a bain-marie for 10 to 12 minutes at 180°C. Serve with toast.
