Asparagus with creamy morels

30 minServes 4Easy

Crisp asparagus coated in morel cream for a delicate starter.

Ingredients

  • 800 g green or white asparagus
  • 30 g dried morels
  • 200 ml cream
  • 1 shallot, 20 g butter and lemon juice

Method

  1. Soak the morels and steam asparagus for 8 minutes.
  2. Cook the shallot and morels in butter.
  3. Add cream, season and reduce for 8 minutes.
  4. Coat the asparagus, add lemon and serve four plates.
