Potato mille-feuille with morel sauce and fresh truffle

1 h 10Serves 4Festive

Thin layers of potato served with morel sauce and freshly shaved truffle.

Ingredients

  • 800 g potatoes
  • 30 g dried morels
  • 200 ml cream
  • 20 g fresh truffle, 30 g butter and 150 ml stock

Method

  1. Soak the morels and slice the potatoes thinly.
  2. Layer the potatoes in a buttered tin and bake for 45 minutes at 180°C.
  3. Simmer the morels with stock and cream for 10 minutes.
  4. Turn out, spoon over sauce and shave truffle for four plates.
