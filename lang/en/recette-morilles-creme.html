Creamy morels

30 minServes 4Easy

A simple, elegant recipe that reveals the full depth of morels in a gentle cream sauce.

Ingredients

  • 40 g dried morels or 250 g fresh morels
  • 250 ml heavy cream
  • 1 shallot, 20 g butter, salt and pepper
  • Fresh parsley

Method

  1. Soak dried morels for 20 minutes, then rinse carefully.
  2. Cook the shallot in butter, add the morels and cook for 8 minutes.
  3. Add cream, season and simmer gently for 10 minutes.
  4. Serve with fresh pasta, rice or poultry.
